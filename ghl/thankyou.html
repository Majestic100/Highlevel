Step #2: Get your questions answered before the call!

Question 01

How many new clients will I get?

Most artists and studios get 10 to 30 new bookings in their first month, and we guarantee 20 in your first 30 days. On the call we run the numbers for your shop: your average session price, your budget and what that adds up to each month.

Question 02

How do you make sure the leads are serious?

The ads and your page are built to show your work and your prices, so people who are just browsing drop off early. We then answer and call every lead ourselves, and a client only lands in your calendar once they've paid a deposit.

Question 03

What if people don't show up?

Every booking is secured with a deposit before it reaches your calendar. People who have paid show up, and if they don't, the deposit covers your time.

Question 04

Do I have to sell anything?

No. We handle the conversation from the first message until the deposit is paid. When the client walks in, the sale is already done. You just tattoo.

Question 05

Is this just a cheap promotion?

No. We don't run discount deals that attract bargain hunters. Your ads show your work and your style, so the people who book want you, at your prices.

Question 06

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. It's month-to-month, and you can cancel anytime with no fees and no fine print. We earn your business every month, or you walk.

Question 07

What if it doesn't work?

If we miss 20 new clients in your first 30 days, you pay us nothing until we deliver them. The guarantee applies with at least $1,000 a month in ad spend. We can promise it because we only take on artists and studios we know we can fill.

Question 08

How fast will I see results?

Most artists and studios get their first deposit-paid booking within 7 days of launch. Inside 30 days you have 20 new clients or more. That's our guarantee, not a hope.

Question 09

What makes you different from other agencies?

We only work with tattoo artists and studios, so the playbook is dialed in. We measure real bookings with paid deposits, not lists of curious people. And we take one artist or studio per area, so we never run ads against you.

Question 10

What's the minimum ad spend?

The guarantee applies from $1,000 a month in ad spend. Most artists and studios run between $1,500 and $5,000 a month, depending on their market and how fast they want to grow. Ad spend goes straight to Meta, not to us.
