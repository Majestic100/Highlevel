Questions studios ask us

How fast will I see real results?
Most studios get their first deposit-secured booking within 7 days of launch, and many are at 30+ booked sessions inside the first 30 days.
What if ads just don't work for my studio?
We vet every studio before we onboard. If we don't believe we can fill your chair, we won't take you on — we only start when we're confident we can get you results.
What kind of ad spend do I need?
Most studios run between $1,500 and $5,000 a month in ad spend. The right number depends on your market and how aggressive you want to be. Ad spend goes directly to the platform, not to us.
What if my market is already taken?
One studio per market is a hard rule. We'll tell you the moment you mention your city — if it's open, we move fast before someone else claims it.
