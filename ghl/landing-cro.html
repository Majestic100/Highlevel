FAQ

Questions, answered

How fast can you start filling my calendar?
Once we have access to your accounts and a bit of content, campaigns are usually live within a few days. The 30-day guarantee window starts from launch.
Do I need to do the marketing myself?
No. We run the ads, write the content, build the landing pages, answer the leads and book them. You show up to the consultations and tattoo.
What does it cost?
You pay a monthly fee for our work, plus your own ad spend directly to Facebook. We go through the exact numbers on the call so there are no surprises.
What if it doesn't work?
If we don't hit 30 deposit-secured booked sessions in your first 30 days, we refund our fee and cover your next month's ad spend. As long as you show up to the consults and follow the pricing we agree on, the risk is ours.
Do you work with more than one studio in my city?
Never. One studio per market. While you're a client we won't take on a competing studio in your area.
