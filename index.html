FAQ

Questions we get all the time.

What does this actually cost? +

It depends on your market size, your goals, and how much ad spend makes sense for your shop. We don't have a public price sheet because every studio is different. On the strategy call we'll give you a straight number based on what you actually need. If it's not a fit on your end financially, we'll say so on the call.

How fast will I see real results? +

Most studios get their first deposit-secured booking within 7 days of launch. Inside 30 days, you should be at 30 booked sessions or more. That's our guarantee, not a hope.

What if ads just don't work for my studio? +

If we miss the 30-in-30 guarantee, we refund our fee and cover your next month's ad spend. The reason we can offer that is we vet every studio before we onboard. If we don't think we can hit the number for your shop, we won't take you on.

How much do I actually have to do? +

Film a handful of short videos a week from the scripts we send you. Show up to your appointments. That's it. We run the ads, build the landing page, set up the booking system, and reply to every lead. You stay at the chair.

Do I have to sign a long contract? +

No. Month-to-month. Cancel anytime, no fees, no fine print. We earn your business every month or you walk.

What makes you different from the agency I tried last year? +

Three things. One, we only work with tattoo studios, so the playbook is dialed in. Two, we measure deposit-secured bookings, not lead form fills, because lead counts don't pay your rent. Three, we lock you in as the only studio in your market, so we're not running the same ads against your competitor down the street.

Where are you based? +

Aarhus, Denmark. We serve tattoo studios across the United States. We work US business hours for our clients. Your bookings don't care where the office is, and neither do we.

Do you work with solo artists or just shops? +

Both. If you've got a chair, a portfolio worth showing, and you can take deposits, we can fill your calendar. Some of our best results are with solo artists running everything alone.

What if my market is already taken? +

Then we won't take you on. One studio per market is a hard rule, no exceptions. Book a call anyway, we'll tell you the moment you mention your city. If your market is open, we'll move fast before someone else claims it.

What kind of ad spend do I need? +

Most of our studios run between $1,500 and $5,000 a month in ad spend. The right number for you depends on your market and how aggressive you want to be. Ad spend goes directly to Meta, not to us.
